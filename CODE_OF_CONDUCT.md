# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions and make technical decisions transparent.
- Respect privacy, coordinated disclosure, and data-handling boundaries.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and correct mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
other spaces where someone represents SigmaMutant.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer using the private
contact method published on the repository owner's profile. Do not include a
conduct report in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and
as promptly and impartially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
